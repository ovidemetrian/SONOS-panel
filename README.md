# SONOS Panel/Controller 

**A dedicated, room-first controller concept for whole-home audio.**
Designed by Ovi Demetrian · Media Content Delivery, LLC · v1.9

**▶ Live demo:** [ovidemetrian.github.io/SONOS-panel](https://ovidemetrian.github.io/SONOS-panel/)
Best experienced on a phone or tablet. No install, no account, no sign-in.

---

## Why this exists

For 30 years I've been the person standing in the living room after the install is done. That taught me one truth: **the final ten feet is everything.** The rack can be flawless, but the experience is decided by the people on the sofa. They don't want to manage a system. They want to live in it.

The original Sonos CR100 controller was retired in 2018 because its battery and processor aged. Its idea never had to be. This concept brings that idea back as a dedicated, room-first controller for the wall, the tablet on the counter, or a guest who has never opened an app. It's rebuilt on hardware people already own.

The app stays the place to set up and manage a system. The panel is for simply living in it.

---

## Design principles

| Principle | What it means in practice |
|---|---|
| **Colour as context** | Every room has its own colour, and the panel takes on the colour of the active room. You know where you are before you read a word. |
| **One screen, whole house** | Room grouping happens on a single screen with no scrolling. The entire home is visible at a glance. |
| **Mistakes are cheap** | Every major action can be reversed. People explore freely when they aren't afraid of breaking the house. |
| **Nothing gets taken away** | Alarms, sleep timers, voice and intercom are part of the core, not add-ons. |

## Features

**Everyday control**
- Now Playing with volume and quick source selection (Music, Scenes)
- Room grouping: this room only, or all rooms together
- Sleep timer that fades music out gradually
- Identify: plays a chime so you can find which speaker is which

**Whole-house actions**
- **Priority One (P1):** silences the entire house instantly and restores everything exactly as it was
- **Back to Square One:** resets every room, with an immediate undo
- **Scenes:** one-touch setups for the whole home

**Voice and intercom**
- Voice control with spoken replies, plus a typed fallback when the microphone isn't available
- Intercom with quick announcements and hold-to-talk. Music in the target rooms lowers while you speak, then comes back.

**Setup and settings**
- Custom rooms, each with its own colour, plus a TV flag for rooms with a soundbar
- Morning alarm
- New-speaker setup flow
- Sample houses (4 or 6 rooms) for demonstration
- Command log for reviewing actions

## Technical notes

- A single self-contained HTML file with no build step, framework or backend
- Runs in any modern mobile or desktop browser
- Voice features depend on the browser's speech support, which varies by browser and device
- **All music, lighting and shade controls are simulated.** This is an interface concept and does not connect to real speakers or smart-home devices.
